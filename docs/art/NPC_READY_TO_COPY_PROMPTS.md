# NPC_READY_TO_COPY_PROMPTS.md

아래 프롬프트는 `공통 프롬프트 없이` 그대로 복사해서 바로 요청할 수 있는 형태로 작성했다.

사용 규칙:
- 핵심 NPC는 `1회 요청 = NPC 1명`으로 쓴다.
- 배회 NPC는 `세트형 요청`으로 쓴다.
- 결과물은 상용 모바일 ARPG 기준의 `대화용 초상 또는 NPC 패키지 아트` 품질을 목표로 한다.

## 1. 루멘 무기상

```text
Create a commercial-quality NPC portrait and full-body design image for Hero Sword, a Korean mobile fantasy ARPG for Android. Character: the weapon merchant of Lumen Village, a practical middle-aged blacksmith-seller who runs the village weapon shop. Show a grounded fantasy craftsman with believable work clothes, leather apron layers, metalworking calluses, and a friendly but no-nonsense expression. The silhouette must read clearly as a weapon merchant without relying on exaggerated fantasy props. Include a clean standing pose suitable for shop UI use and a readable chest-up dialogue portrait feel. Keep the finish polished and production-ready, with natural costume logic, restrained ornament, and no obvious AI-generated anatomy or costume join errors.
```
